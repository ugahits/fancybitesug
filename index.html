A LITTLE ABOUT US

We bake with purpose.

Fancy Bites Enterprises Ltd is a Ugandan baking business built around a simple idea: make bakery products people can enjoy while keeping sugar minimized.

From breads and cakes to pastries and cookies, we focus on good presentation, consistent quality, customer care and a fresh experience from our bakery to your table.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE BRAND

Meet Our Co-Founders

Five people. Different strengths. One shared vision for Fancy Bites.

Kyagaba Enoch

Branding and Marketing Manager

Enoch helps shape the Fancy Bites identity and connects the brand with customers. His work covers branding, promotions, digital communication and marketing. He believes a good product becomes memorable when its story is clear, creative and consistent.

Steve U. Lauben

Operational Manager

Steve focuses on the day-to-day operations that keep Fancy Bites moving. He supports coordination, teamwork and smooth working processes. His practical approach helps turn plans into action and keeps the team focused on consistent service.

Atuhaire Mariat

Finance Manager

Mariat oversees the financial side of the business, including budgeting, records, expenses and planning. Her attention to detail supports responsible use of resources and helps Fancy Bites plan for steady, sustainable growth.

Kakuru Enos

Sales Manager

Enos leads sales and customer relationships. He works to connect Fancy Bites products with customers, identify opportunities and grow the market. His focus is simple: understand customers, serve them well and build lasting relationships.

Atukunda Madinah

Secretary

Madinah supports communication, records, coordination and administration. Her organization and attention to detail help the team stay informed and prepared, giving Fancy Bites a strong foundation for effective teamwork.
